# AxKit.Forms: the shared controls, keys and sounds

Part of the notes of `src/AxKit.Forms` (the index is `src/AxKit.Forms/CLAUDE.md`). One bullet per piece.

- `EventDoor` (docs/axKit/events.md EV-5, 2026-11-17): the one place that plays a sound. A window or a control raises the kind of event that happened, `EventDoor.Raise(EventKind.Edge)`, and the kind's sound comes from the one table (`AxKit.Core/Events/EventPresenter`). A kind with one sound is raised by its name alone; the three kinds that still have two (nothing-found, no-command, program-asks) are raised with the sound named, `EventDoor.Raise(EventKind.NothingFound, EventSound.Edge)`, until the maintainer has decided each by ear. The two sounds that last have calls of their own, `Working(on)` (the music) and `BackgroundRunning(chord, things)`; `Muted` and `TurnDoneThroughMute()` are for a window put away. Whether an event is raised at all (a switch of the user's, a window behind) is decided where it happens, as before, and the words are still the window's `Announce`, in the order each place had. A notice says its kind itself (`Notice.Kind`: by itself, a failure, a pairing), and the window base raises it. A raise is written whole on one line with the kind by name, never a variable: `EventDoorTests` reads the sources for it, and `tools/families.ps1` fails when a file raises a kind that does not name the file in its "where" (docs/axKit/families.json). A new moment needs its kind first, in `EventKind` and in that file; a sound is given to a kind in the table, by the maintainer's ear.
- `Sounds`: internal to this project since the door, which is its one caller (the automation channel listens to `Played`); an app cannot name it. The chimes and ticks through one winmm wave-out device kept open for the life of the process, every sound prepared once and queued with a single write, and axClaude's working music as one buffer that loops with the loop flags on a second device, so that a chime's reset never cuts it (see the gotchas in `src/AxClaude/CLAUDE.md`). `Notice()` is the one every window uses; `Edge()` (a short low note, 2026-10-22; two falling an octave and louder since 2026-10-23, the one note lost under speech) is the earcon for a key that moves nothing or a typed name nothing starts with (axFile AF-3.6), where a sentence would be noise. `Offer()` (five short swoops falling from 2400 to 900 Hz, an alarm, `WaveTone.Swoops`, chosen by ear; 2026-11-13) is the chime of a command offered for the user to run (axClaude FR-2.12), in place of `Notice()`, so that an offer is told from a question before a word is spoken. `Heading()` (G4 leaping to G5, 85 ms, AX-7.9, 2026-11-11) is played by the window's `SoundOnHeading` (`AxKitForm.Theme.cs`, unless Options, Text, Sound on headings is off, `display.headingSound`, TH-6.6) when the user's key lands on a heading: the item apps' `ItemForm` from `AxKitList.UserMovedSelection` on a section row, the editors with Markdown headings and every notice's text through `FollowHeadings` from `AxKitTextBox.UserMovedLine`.
- `EditKeys.Page`: Page Up and Page Down in an edit control, one screen at a time and to the ends (`EM_LINESCROLL` against `EM_GETFIRSTVISIBLELINE`, since `EM_SCROLLCARET` is ignored without the focus).
- `EditKeys.Paragraph`: Ctrl+Up and Ctrl+Down in an edit control move to the start of the previous or next paragraph (the first line, or a line with text after a blank line), and with Shift grow the selection to there (AX-8.2). The EDIT control's own keys scroll a row and leave the caret, which a screen reader reads as the same text again. The arithmetic is `AxKit.Core.Paragraphs`, tested. The control hides the selection's anchor: the caret is drawn at the active end, so `GetCaretPos` against the position of the selection's start (`GetPositionFromCharIndex`) tells which end moves, and `EM_SETSEL` takes the anchor first and the caret second.
- `EditKeys`: the KeyDown handler that gives any edit control the two above; since 2026-11-09 nothing subscribes it: `AxKitTextBox` calls it itself, after the window's own handlers, for a multi-line control (`TextBoxTests` fails on a subscription, which would move the caret twice).
- `AxKitTextBox` (AX-8.9, 2026-11-09): the edit control of every app, a `TextBox` with no length cap and the edit keys above built in, after the window's own KeyDown handlers, so that a window's key on the same stroke comes first (axTerminal's Ctrl+Up is its history); `TranscriptView` derives from it. Word wrap is the control's (on); the editors alone take it from the theme file (`AxKitForm.Theme.cs`, TH-6). A line break a key puts into an editable field of several lines is said, "new line" (AX-7.8, 2026-11-11): the text and selection are kept as Enter goes down, compared after a handler that suppressed the key press or after the control's `WM_CHAR` for Enter or Ctrl+Enter (`AxKit.Core.TypedLineBreak`), and the window's `SayTypedLineBreak` announces it unless Options, Text, Say new line is off (`display.sayNewLine` in the theme file, TH-6.5; the item is placed by `CreateTextMenu` in every window and checked from the theme read at the last activation as the Text menu opens). Since AX-7.9 (2026-11-11) `UserMovedLine`: the caret's line before and after a key's handling (`PreProcessMessage` for the window's commands, `WndProc` for the control's own move), raised when the line changed with the text unchanged and nothing selected; `AxKitForm.FollowHeadings` plays the heading sound from it on a Markdown heading's line (`MarkdownOutline.HeadingLineAt`), once per heading. `TextBoxTests` fails on a plain `TextBox` made in `src`.
  - Edit mode and navigation mode (AX-8.13, 2026-11-15): `HasModes` (multi-line and not read-only, the rule, so a new field of several lines has them by being made), `NavigationMode` (off at every start, never saved), `ToggleMode` and `ModeChanged` (the editors and the console windows put the mode in the status bar from it), `NavigationJumps` (Markdown's letters, `TextJump.Letters(MarkdownKeys.Jumps)`, unless the window gives its own). In navigation mode the box's `ProcessCmdKey` hands every key down to the window base's `TakeNavigationKey` (`AxKitForm.Modes.cs`) before its own shortcuts (Ctrl+Backspace is the text box's) and the window's commands: jump letters, the paragraph keys (ahead of axTerminal's history), and a refused key with `Sounds.Edge` (`AxKit.Core.TextModes.InNavigation`). `WndProc` stops a character (`TextModes.CharacterAllowed`), `WM_PASTE`, `WM_CUT`, `WM_CLEAR` and the undo messages with the edge sound: Tab in an editor and a character a program sends come that way. The mode key itself is the base's `TextModeKey` from `ProcessCmdKey`, for the control the key is addressed to (never in menu mode): `ToggleMode`, the read-only line for a read-only text, the edge sound elsewhere. A menu command that changes the text asks `RefusedInNavigation` first (the editors' Undo, Redo, Cut, Paste, `RunCommand`). The key test says the mode's keys through `KeyTestInMode`.
- `WindowsOpenWith` (2026-10-24; from axFile's `Shell.OpenWith` on its second use, axPreview's Open with): Windows' "How do you want to open this file?" dialog through `SHOpenWithDialog`, the program chosen running the file and the dialog's own Always setting the default; the reason when Windows refuses.
- `User32` (2026-10-24): the Win32 calls and messages more than one file makes (`SendMessage`, `PostMessage`, the foreground and window-process calls, the key and edit messages), declared once and used through `using static AxKit.Forms.User32;`; a call only one file makes stays declared next to its use.
- `Announcer`: the one UI Automation notification (AX-7.1): "most recent" for the result of the user's own key, "all" for status; false without a handle, so the caller can fall back to a selection. Every window and the notice announce through it.
- `ListSelection` and `SelectionKeys` (AX-8.16; from axFile's window on its second use, the item windows, 2026-11-22): selecting several rows of an `AxKitList`, the same in every window.
  - `SelectionKeys` is the one table of the keys (Space, Shift+Space, Shift with Up, Down, Home and End, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+Shift+A, Escape) with their names; a window's key table includes `All`, or leaves out the keys that have a second meaning in another of its panes and names both in an entry of its own (`WithoutSpaceAndEscape`).
  - `ListSelection` is one list's selection: the `AxKit.Core/Marks` on the rows the window gives it, `Label` (a row's words with ", selected" while it is, which the window fills its items with), `Toggle`, `ToHere`, `All`, `None`, `Extend` and `Restore`, each applied one way: the items whose mark flipped rewritten in place, `Recorded` called for a window that keeps a record (axFile's Actions list), the words said once. `MoveCommands` makes the four commands that select as they move, with the window's word for a row. `Keep` when the same level is filled again, `Clear` for another level; `Count`, `Contains` and `Keys` say what the window's commands act on.
  - The list stays a single-selection list box and the marks are text in the items (axFile's AF-D9). Setting an item's text reinserts the native item; only the flipped rows are touched.
- `AxKitList` (AX-8.6, 2026-10-24): the list of every app, a `ListBox` with type-to-find and the edge earcon built in.
  - Type-to-find: letters within a second one prefix through `AxKit.Core/TypeAhead`, Shift with a letter backwards from `ModifierKeys`, the edge earcon when nothing starts with it, `e.Handled` so that the list box's own one-letter jump never runs; any other move of the selection starts a new search, `OnSelectedIndexChanged` with `_jumping` set around the search's own move, since axAutomate typed "s", moved, then "b" within the second and searched "sb"; a space never starts a search.
  - The edge earcon for Up or Home on the first item and Down or End on the last (`OnKeyDown`, after the window's `ProcessCmdKey` has had its keys).
  - `UserMovedSelection` (AX-7.9, 2026-11-11): raised when the selection moves while the list handles a key (`PreProcessMessage`, where the form's commands run, and `WndProc` for the list box's own move and type-to-find), never inside `Fill` or `SetLines`; the item apps play the heading sound from it. `SelectForCommand` selects a row for a window's command the user asked for (a section or heading jump) as a key's move, so that the menu, the palette and an AI tool's run get the heading sound too (2026-11-16, found by the AI tools mode of the checks).
  - `Names` when some items are not names, `ResetTypeAhead` for a list filled anew.
  - `SetLines` (2026-10-25; from axMedia's queue and axFile's Actions list, inventory item 7): the lines made again, each changed line rewritten in place while the count is the same (a line the caller names left alone), the whole list otherwise, true when in place.
  - The rename in place (AX-8.7; from axFile on its second use, axTask and axPass, 2026-10-24):
    - `BeginRename` puts a "New name" box over the selected item, Enter hands its text to the window's commit (which calls `EndRename` when it takes it), Escape and leaving the box end it, `ended` tells the window;
    - `Renaming` lets a window's commands leave the box its own keys (Ctrl+Z, in a `When`);
    - the box sets its selection before it takes the focus (a `TextBox` given the focus selects all its text unless a selection was set first), and `EndRename` gives the focus back to the list before hiding the box, or WinForms picks the next control itself.
  - Where it is: in axFile's folder and Actions lists, axGIT's list, axMedia's queue, axProcess's list, the item apps' navigator and axCalc's history; `tests/AxKit.Tests/ListTests` fails on a plain `ListBox` in an app, its exceptions named there (the lists that never take a typed letter).
  - The automation channel's `keys` make no typed character and its `type` no Shift, so the backward search is in the unit tests and the NVDA plans.
- `AxKitCheckBox` (AX-8.8, 2026-10-31): the check box of every app, a `CheckBox` that says "checked" or "not checked" when the user toggles it, on the click alone (Space and the mouse), never for a change the window makes (NVDA said nothing on Space in the consent notice's box); `tests/AxKit.Tests/CheckBoxTests` fails on a plain `CheckBox`. In the notice's consent box, whose check the panel keeps for the same notice shown again after Ctrl+F.
- `ChoiceItems<T>` (AX-6.4, 2026-11-09): a menu of choices of which one is checked, the items made from the choices and re-checked by `Check`; from axFile's Sort by, axProcess's Sort processes by, axClaude's Speak replies, axTerminal's Speak output, axPass's Lock after and axCalc's Angles, each window keeping the click's work and its words.
- `PrivateClipboard` (security audit S-15; docs/axPass/SPEC.md PW-5.4, 2026-10-24): a secret on the clipboard with the marks password managers set (no clipboard history, no cloud clipboard, no clipboard viewers), cleared by the clipboard's sequence number so that a later copy of the user's is left alone; axPass's copies of a password and a user name.
- `WindowAccessibleObject`: every window's accessible object, reporting the client area with the title bar as the window's rectangle, so that NVDA+End finds the status bar (NVDA looks at the bottom-left pixel of the rectangle UI Automation gives, which for a WinForms window lies in the invisible resize border).
- `StatusLayout`: `Pair` makes a window's two status labels (no AccessibleName, no Spring, kept inside the strip as it resizes); `Fit` gives them fixed widths when their texts do not fit (an unexposed label otherwise).
- `BundleKeys` (AX-8.2): the keys that mean the same in every window; `MainAction` is Ctrl+Enter (AX-8.10 rule 3, 2026-11-14), which a window with a main action registers a command on; `SayStatus` is F8 (AX-7.10, 2026-11-15), handled by the base for every window, in a notice too; `ToggleMode` is Ctrl+Space, the mode key (AX-8.13, 2026-11-15), one line so that the user can have it changed; `AskClaudeAlso` is Ctrl+Slash, a further key of Shift+F1's command, and `ContextMenu` is Ctrl+Period, which the base turns into what Shift+F10 does where the focus is (AX-8.15, 2026-11-16: the window's command on Shift+F10, else a posted `WM_CONTEXTMENU` to the focused control; `OpenBarMenu` waits for Ctrl and the period key to be up too; the window's command listing gives both as further keys, `CommandRows`); `All` feeds the collision test; `SystemWide` (AX-8.11, 2026-11-14) lists the background apps' hotkeys as they are at first and the one kept for later, which no table may hold (`KeyTests`); `Text` spells any key the way a menu shows it ("Ctrl+Shift+S"), so that both windows' menu items read their shortcut text from the tables.
- `Sounds.BackgroundStart`, `Sounds.BackgroundFinish` and `Sounds.BackgroundChord` (axClaude FR-7.10, 2026-11-15; each chosen by the user by ear that day): work in the background started (three quick bell notes up with a sparkle), ended (the same three down), and still runs (the recurring chords: one slow soft chord of 4.6 s with a reverb tail, of the working music's four, with a tone more for each thing running, five at most; when one plays is axClaude's `BackgroundChords`, not this class). The samples are made in `AxKit.Core/Audio/AmbientSounds`, one method each and the one place a sound is made, held by `AmbientSoundsTests` to the samples he heard, so change none without his ear (`BackgroundStart`, `BackgroundFinish`, `BackgroundChord`), as is the working music (`WorkingMusic`, a pad that loops); `Sounds` keeps them as `BackgroundStartTone`, `BackgroundFinishTone` and the table `BackgroundChords`. The start and the finish play behind whatever plays (`replace: false`), so they never cut a chime, and a chime cuts them. A chord takes 13 to 30 ms to make, so it is made at its first use on a pool thread (`PlayChord`: Task.Run, then posted back to the UI thread's `SynchronizationContext`; `BackgroundChordsBeingMade` keeps a second request from making it twice), kept, added to its device (`Device.Add`, which prepares it at once when the device is open) and played then, unless `Muted` came meanwhile; the timeline records "BackgroundChord 3" when it is asked for, and a test run makes no chord at all. The chords have a third device of their own (`ChordDevice`, the same `Device` class): on the chimes' device every tick would wait behind a chord; `Muted` resets it with the music.
- `Sounds.Tool` (axClaude FR-7.8): the tool call's sound, `ToolPop`, a tiny mouth pop of 30 ms (`WaveTone.Pop(0.3, 700, 300, 0.006, 0.030)`), chosen by the user by ear on 2026-11-15 in place of the two knocks of noise and tone; queued on the chimes' device like the line tick (`replace: false`), dropped while its own buffer is queued.
- `Sounds.NavKey`, `Sounds.Nothing` and `Sounds.Unassigned` (AX-8.13, 2026-11-16): the three sounds of a plain key in a text's navigation mode, played by `AxKitForm.TakeNavigationKey`. `NavKey` is the high glassy tick of a letter whose command has somewhere to go (`WaveTone.Tick(0.3, 3800, 3800, 0.006, 10, 700, 0.0)`); `Nothing` is a low buzz when the command finds nothing of its kind, in place of the tick (`WaveTone.Flat(0.3, 150, 110, 6, buzzy: true)`); `Unassigned` is a wooden bonk for a key that would type and has no command (`WaveTone.Knock(0.4, 420, 260, 0.008, 0.022, 2.76, 0.5, 70)`, the rule is `TextModes.Unassigned`). All three were chosen by the maintainer by ear on 2026-11-16; change them only with his ear, and the candidates heard are kept in `docs/axKit/earcons.md`. `TextJump.Jump` tells its `before` whether it found something before it moves or speaks, which is how one key gives one sound. All three replace what plays on the chimes' device, as `Edge` does, so the heading sound that follows a landing on a heading cuts the tick. `Edge` stays for a refused edit and for the ends of lists and texts.
- `Sounds.Muted` (2026-11-15, docs/axClaude/SPEC.md FR-9.6): no sound at all while the process's one window is put away, hidden by its close and running on; a sound asked for meanwhile is neither played nor recorded, the music stops and starts again when the mute ends while it is still wanted. `ForegroundReturn`: the hotkey windows' rule of the foreground as a class (`notes/hotkey.md`).

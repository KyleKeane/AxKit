# AxKit.Forms: the item window, the bundle's navigator (axTask, axPass, axMCP, axAutomate, axMedia, axProcess, axGIT, axFile)

Part of the notes of `src/AxKit.Forms` (the index is `src/AxKit.Forms/CLAUDE.md`). One bullet per piece.

- `ContextBarMenu` (AX-9.11, B-19, 2026-11-11): the bar's Context menu over a window's menus of what acts on each kind of thing (the item apps' list, item and field; axProcess's three views), none of them on the bar and each disabled: `Fill` moves the items of the one the window's function names into Context and puts the ones shown before back, as Context opens, as the palette reads it and as an AI tool asks (`RefreshedOnOpening` on Context; the held menus stay off the bar and disabled, and every reader reads the bar alone, `CommandPalette.Entries`); with nothing to act on, one enabled item saying why; `Letter` is C.
- The views of one list are gone (2026-11-22, docs/axKit/framework.md FW-4.6): axGIT and axProcess, which each kept three views of their one list in a shared record of a view's name, labels, keys and row, are navigators whose three contents are three levels, and the record was deleted with the last of them; it is in the git history.
- `ItemForm` and `ItemKeys`, with the records `ItemRow`, `ItemDetail` and `ItemDetailLine` (docs/axKit/item-apps.md; from axTask on its second use, axPass, 2026-10-24): the item apps' window on `AxKitForm`.
  - `ItemForm.cs`:
    - the navigator and the detail pane (`Navigator`, `DetailPane`, each an `AxKitList`) in a `SplitContainer`, the status bar (`SetPlace` changes its second part alone, for a state that changes while the level stays: axTask's "Running" and an action's name);
    - `PlaceControls` with the app's menu bar, the commands of `ItemKeys` registered after the app's own (`AddSharedKeys`, called by `PlaceControls`; the bundle's Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+Shift+Tab and F6 switch the panes from either one, AX-8.2, 2026-11-09);
    - the levels (`Into`, `OpenSelected`, `UpLevel`, `AnnounceLevel`, `Refresh`);
    - `Fill` (the one place the navigator, its name, the title and the status bar are filled, from the app's `ReadLevel`), and `Refill` for a change off the user's command (axTask's projects found, 2026-11-11: the rows rewritten in place and the selection kept while the same keys show, the detail pane made again only when the selected row's words changed and, while it has the focus, once the focus reaches the navigator (`_detailStale`), `Fill` otherwise; `LevelIdentity` names the level for `McpTarget`, the place by default);
    - and `FillDetail` (silently, on every selection change, from the app's `DetailOf`; the field selected kept while the same `ItemDetail.Key` shows);
    - the selection of several rows (AX-8.16, 2026-11-22): `Selection`, the bundle's `ListSelection` on the navigator, over `_markRows` (each row by its key and name, selectable when the app `HasSelection`, the row is no heading and `CanSelectRow` holds). `Fill` and `Refill` call `KeepSelection` before the items are made (the marks kept while `LevelIdentity` is the same, dropped for another level) and make each item with `RowText` (", selected" while it is); `ShowName` puts ", 3 selected" into the status bar's first part. Space is the command "Select or deselect" in every item window (`SelectRow`: the edge sound through `NothingToSelect` where nothing can be selected; until 2026-11-22 the command "Check", axTask's Done, which is Ctrl+Enter there now); the other keys of `SelectionKeys` are commands only where the app `HasSelection` (`SelectToHere`, the four `MoveCommands`, `SelectAllRows`, `SelectNoRows`, and Escape while rows are selected). `SelectedRows` is what the app's commands act on, `KeyAfter` the row to select when rows leave the level, and `McpTarget` names the selection with the row while there is one.
    - what a navigator may do without (docs/axKit/framework.md FW-4.7, step 4, 2026-11-22; for the windows that move onto the type, nothing of it changes the four item apps): `Pane`, the kind of second pane (`SecondPane`: `Fields`, the default, the detail pane of the row you are on; `None` shows the navigator alone: the split's second panel collapsed, the pane keys and the detail pane's commands not made, `FillDetail` a no-op, Enter on an item the edge unless the app gives it a command; `Text`, for axGIT, step 8: `TextPane`, the bundle's read-only text, under the navigator, which the app fills with `ShowText` (from axGIT's own method for its details: the name, the line ends, the caret on the first line, the focus there when asked, "name, n lines" said), Tab, Shift+Tab and the bundle's focus keys go to it and back, Escape comes back, and Ctrl+F, F3 and Shift+F3 find in it while it has the focus; `HasDetailPane` was its name while it was yes or no); `PlaceWith(menu, controls)`, as `PlaceControls` with further controls of the app's own docked between the panes and the status bar, which the app hides around a notice itself; `StatusState` (what the status bar's first part says in place of the level's name) with `ShowStatus` to show it again; `EmptyLine` (the one line a level without rows shows, which is no row, said by `WithRow` and Enter); and in `AddSharedKeys` a shared key is not registered when the app gave that key to a command that takes it there (`AxKitForm.KeyTakenWhere`), so that no listed command is one that cannot run. With axMedia's move (step 6, the same day): `OpenItem` (Enter on an item, the one key the apps of the type differ in: the fields take the focus by default, axMedia plays the file), `RowNoun` (the app's word for a row in the names of the commands that select as they move), `CreateContextMenu(menuOf, menus)` for a navigator without the pane whose rows are of the app's own kinds (Context holds the menu `menuOf` gives for the row you are on: axMedia's one, axProcess's four; it says the `EmptyLine` while the level is empty), `KeysWithoutUse` (the navigator's keys an app has no use for are no commands of its window: axMedia's F2, Ctrl+Z, F5 and Ctrl+Shift+N), the pane keys as "Back to the list" without the second pane, an empty title from `ReadLevel` as the app's name alone, and defaults for what a navigator of one level without the pane never needs (`EnterList`, `LeaveList`, `DetailOf`, `SaveFields`, `RenameItem`, `RefusedName`). With axFile's move (step 13, the same day): `SecondPane.Log`, the pane as a list of lines the app keeps (a log of its operations at first, axFile's Favorites list since 2026-11-23; the pane is `DetailPane`, a list the app fills and names itself, under the navigator with a third of the height; no command of the fields is made and `FillDetail` is a no-op; Tab, Shift+Tab and the focus keys go to it and back, Escape comes back, the two menu keys open Context, which holds `LogLineMenu` while the log was the last of the two to have the focus; what Enter and the other keys do on a line is the app's command, registered with `InDetail` before the shared keys; `LogName` is the app's word for it in those commands' names; its type-ahead goes over its own lines); `AfterRowsLine` (a line after the rows that is no row, in `Fill` and `Refill` through `LevelLines`), with `NoRowSaid` (what a command that needs a row says on a line that is none: that line, which Context holds too); `UpLevel` virtual, for an app whose level above is read off the UI thread; `RenameSelectionLength` (how much of the name the rename box selects as it opens: all of it by default, axFile a file's name before its extension); and in `AddSharedKeys` a shared key is also left to the app where the app gave it to a command of its own in the very place the shared one acts, the same condition (`AxKitForm.KeyTakenWhere`): a window moves onto the type with its own commands first and gives them up one by one.
  - `ItemForm.Arrange.cs` (docs/axKit/framework.md FW-4.10a, 2026-11-22; the maintainer: "i want any list to be able to be filtered or sorted"; from axFile's window and axProcess's sort menu): the filter and the sort of every navigator. `AddArrangeItems` puts Filter… and Sort by at the top of the window's Options menu as the controls are placed (`PlaceWith`), found on the bar by its name, so that no app adds them and none can forget them; neither has a key. `Fill` and `Refill` pass what `ReadLevel` gives through `Arranged` (the chosen order, then the filter; the rules are `AxKit.Core/ListArrange`, tested: by name with lists first, a heading kept in its place and kept while a row under it passes) and `ArrangedName` ("Home, 4 items, filter tap, 1 shown"), so `Rows` is what the list shows and an app that needs a row's place in its own data keeps it in the row's `Tag` (axMedia's place in the queue). The filter lives in one level and is dropped with it (`LevelIdentity`); a level whose rows all fail it shows one line, "No names contain …" (`EmptyLineNow`, before the app's `EmptyLine`); Escape in the navigator clears it once nothing is selected (`AddArrangeKeys`). The sort: `SortNames` (the orders by their words, the first the one a window starts in; by default the list's own order and name; one name alone means no Sort by menu), `SortChoice` (kept for the window by default; an app with orders of its own keeps it in its settings), `OrdersRowsItself` (the app gives its rows in the chosen order: axFile, axProcess, and axMedia, whose Name reorders the queue itself) and `SortChanged` (the app puts its kept rows in the new order before the list is filled again); "Sorted by name" is said.
  - `ItemForm.Work.cs` (framework FW-4.7 point 1, step 3, 2026-11-23; the maintainer: "build the common one"; from axGIT's and axProcess's busy fields and axFile's read counter): the work a navigator does off the UI thread, under two rules of which an app takes the one its work needs. One piece of work at a time: `BeginWork(what)` with `EndWork` in a finally, and `RefusedWhileWorking`, which says "Working…" to a command that arrives meanwhile (axGIT's every git command, its rule AG-8; axProcess's reads and changes). A read whose late answer is dropped: `BeginRead` gives a ticket and `EndRead(ticket)` says whether it is still the latest (axFile's folder). Either shows as `Working`, the default `StatusState`: the app's words, or `ReadingState`. Nothing is said by itself.
  - `ItemForm.Form.cs` (bundle AX-8.10 rule 17, 2026-11-23; the maintainer: "a form is a form and a detail pane list is a detail pane list and the item list is an item list"; from axTask's `TaskForm.ItemForm.cs` on its second use, axTemplate): the item form, one for every app of the window. An app overrides `FormOf` (a row's `ItemFormSource`: name, document, the same the detail pane shows, its save, and links, a refusal, a note) and, for dates, `FormToday`; the shared Enter commands then open it (`OpenFormOfSelected` on a row, `OpenFormAtField` on a field of the pane, falling back to `OpenSelected` and `ChangeField` where a row has no form). `OpenItemForm` makes the controls from `AxKit.Core/ItemFormRules`, keys each by its field's name and number, makes two or more checks in a row one `FormCheckList` with Check all before it (rule 19, the source's `ChecksName`), a start in one of them selecting its line, checks the rules' refusal and the source's before Save, and `SaveItemForm` says "Nothing changed" or hands the changed document and the words of `ItemFormRules.Said` to the source.
  - `ItemForm.Fields.cs`:
    - every field command (`CheckField`, `ChangeField` by type, with the app's `ChangeSpecial` for its own types and the Title's change through `RenameItem`; `ReadAll`), each a new `ItemDocument` handed to the app's `SaveFields`; no command adds, renames or removes a field any more (2026-11-23, the maintainer's decision): Ctrl+N, F2 and Delete in the pane of fields play the knock of a key without a command (the override of `ProcessCmdKey`, after the commands have had the key);
    - `ReadAll` (Ctrl+Q, "Read in full"): in the navigator the row and all the detail pane shows of it, every field of an item or, for a row whose details are lines and no fields (axAutomate's steps and workflows), every line (2026-11-22; such a row was read alone before, where axAutomate's F1 text said every line); in the detail pane the field or the line you are on.
    - `StepField` (IA-5.6, AX-8.10 rule 4, 2026-11-14): Right and Left in the detail pane, a choice's next and previous value and a check checked and unchecked through `AxKit.Core/FieldStep`, saved and said as every change (`SetValue`), the edge sound on anything else; until then both keys played the edge sound alone;
    - and `ChooseFromList`, the list notice both apps use, with `ChangeLongText`, a long text's box, where Enter is a new line and Ctrl+Enter keeps the text (IA-5.7; `AskText`, the one-field notice, is the base's since 2026-11-09, `AxKitForm.Notices.cs`).
  - The app fills in:
    - the levels (`ReadLevel`, `EnterList`, `LeaveList`; `EntryRefused` for a list Right and Enter do not go into, its reason said, axMCP's remote server; an empty reason says nothing, the app having asked something itself, axTask's question before a list of the earlier format is converted);
    - the detail (`DetailOf`, `SpokenValue`);
    - the saving;
    - the navigator's commands (`NewItem`, `NewList`, `RenameItem`, `RenameTo` (an item's rename by default), `RenameRefused`, `DeleteRow`, `Undo`); `DeleteRow` alone must be written, the others say "Nothing is made here", "Nothing is renamed here" and "Nothing to undo here" by default, and an app that has no use for a key leaves it out with `KeysWithoutUse` rather than override it (2026-11-24, R-90);
    - whether its navigator has a selection (AX-8.16, 2026-11-22): `HasSelection` (no by default; axTask says yes), `CanSelectRow` (which rows; a heading never), `NothingToSelect` (its words on a row that cannot be selected; the edge sound by default);
    - and its three menus of a list, an item and a field, never on the bar, handed to `CreateContextMenu`, which makes the bar's Context from them (AX-9.11, 2026-11-11: the field's in the detail pane, the item's or the list's in the navigator by the pane that last had the focus, `_detailLast`; "Nothing selected" or "A heading: nothing to act on" otherwise; `SelectionMenusAvailable` and `NothingWhileUnavailable` for axPass's lock); Shift+F10 opens it.
  - `ItemKeys.All` is in each app's key table.

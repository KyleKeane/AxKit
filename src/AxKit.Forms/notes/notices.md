# AxKit.Forms: the notices, the forms and the command palette

Part of the notes of `src/AxKit.Forms` (the index is `src/AxKit.Forms/CLAUDE.md`). One bullet per piece. Split from `notes/window-base.md` on 2026-11-22, when that file reached its size limit.

- `FormControls` with `AxKitForm.ShowForm` (docs/axKit/SPEC.md AX-8.10, 2026-11-14): the one piece every form is made of. `FormControls` is a panel of standard controls in Tab order, each under its caption and named with an instruction: `AddText` (one line; Enter presses the default button), `AddLines` (several lines; Enter makes a new line unless it is only read), `AddList` (a choice of one, an `AxKitList`), `AddCheck` (an `AxKitCheckBox`); `FocusFirst`; `EnterMakesNewLine`; each field's keys, `Key` (the item form keys a field by its name and its number), `Field`, `FieldKeys`, `Focus` (its text selected when asked) and `StartIn` by control or by key (AX-8.10 rule 16, 2026-11-23); Ctrl+Down and Ctrl+Up between the fields from any control, the edge sound at the ends, in `ProcessCmdKey`, which takes the paragraph keys of a box of several lines in a form (rule 15); the form's navigation mode on the mode key (rule 18, `Navigation`, `NavigationKey`: Down and Up, type-ahead over the fields' names through `AxKit.Core/TypeAhead`, a number through the keys, Enter back to typing, any key that would type refused with the blips; off on `OnLeave`); `KeysLine`, the keys every form has, which `ShowForm` puts at the end of every explanation. `ShowForm` shows them in a notice under the form's explanation with the default button and Cancel, puts the focus in the first control, says the title and "form" after it, and asks the window's `missing` before the button acts: the control that still needs something takes the focus with the edge sound and its words, the form kept. Whatever asks more than one thing for one action is one such form, never one question after another: the report, axTerminal's Add a shell, axMCP's Add server, the item window's New field, axTask's Action keys and Move to section. A one-field question stays `AskText`. A form made as a list of fields with a Send row was built and withdrawn on 2026-11-14 after the user tried it (AX-8.10 rule 13): never a list.
- `FormCheckList` (AX-8.10 rule 19, 2026-11-23): a group of checks of one kind as one `AxKitList`: one Tab stop, Space checks the line (`OnKeyDown`, the state said alone), Ctrl+A and Ctrl+Shift+A all and none (`SetAll`), `IsChecked`, `ItemNames`, `SelectName`; the lines say "checked" or "not checked", type-to-find over the names alone. `FormControls.AddCheckList` and `AddButton` (Check all, Uncheck all) place it in a form. `FormRadioList` (rule 20): a choice of one as a list, Space chooses (`Choose`, `Chosen`), the arrows move without choosing, "chosen" or "not chosen" on each line; `FormControls.AddRadioList`.
- `ReportControls` (reports.md RP-3): the report form's controls on `FormControls`: "Your report", several lines; "Kind", a list; the check box for more detail; "What is sent with your report", one text that is only read, filled by `Fill` from `AxKit.Core/Reports/ReportPrivacy.Shown` with the plain form (RP-4.2a) or, while the box is checked, the fuller one. `ToReport` builds from the context the box shows, never from a text, and the title is the first line of the words; `Missing` asks for words, "Type a few words first".
- `OverlayPanel` and the records `Notice`, `OverlayChoice`, `OverlayInput`: a window's own dialogs drawn inside the window in place of its main controls (axClaude D23, bundle AX-7.2).
  - The window shows one with `Populate`, which adds the key line made from the buttons ("Enter: Save. Escape: Cancel.", the whole text when the notice has none), sets its `AcceptButton` and `CancelButton` to `DefaultButton` and `CancelButton`, calls `FocusStart`, hides its other controls and disables its menu, and swallows its own shortcuts while the notice shows; `Chosen` tells it which button was pressed.
  - The text and the Custom arguments field have the bundle's page and paragraph keys (`EditKeys`).
  - An `OverlayInput` may carry a `KeyHook`, asked about each key in the field first (axFile's path completion, 2026-10-22), with `InputCaret` and `SetInputText` for the window.
  - A `Notice` with `Progress` shows a progress bar under the text, moved with `SetProgress`; NVDA reports it by its own settings.
  - A `Notice` with `Consent` (AX-11.4, 2026-10-29) shows an `AxKitCheckBox` with that sentence as its name between the text and the buttons; the default button and a `NeedsConsent` choice say "Check the box first" until it is checked; the check is kept, by the sentence, for the same notice shown again after Ctrl+F in it, and forgotten when the notice is answered.
  - A `Notice` may carry a control of the window's own under the text (`Custom`, with `Accepts` asked before the default button acts and `HeadLine`, a first line the window changes through `SetHeadLine`; `PressDefault` for the control's Enter; `PressMain`, Ctrl+Enter, the default button from whichever control has the focus, AX-8.10; `KeyLine` false for a notice whose text says its keys itself, since Enter is a new line where its focus starts: the report form and a long text's box):
    - the New session controls of axClaude and axTerminal are `SessionControls` with `SessionPreset`, in their own file since 2026-10-23, each app giving it its own wording, its folder picker and its announcer (they sat in the panel because it moved whole from axClaude).
  - The text moves by Markdown structure through `AxKit.Core.MarkdownOutline` with the pairs in `Jumps`, the bundle's heading letters (`TextJump.HeadingLetters`) unless the window sets more, announcing on the notice (AX-7.2).
  - `Shown` is the notice on view and `Body` its text, so that the window base's find can put the Find notice in its place and bring it back with the match selected (`HasSearchableText`: a text without a field or a control of the window's own).
- `CommandPalette` (AX-10, 2026-10-22; the one source of every reader since 2026-11-13, docs/axKit/app-kit.md AK-2.5):
  - `Entries` reads the menu bar into `MenuEntry` records (the item, its name, its menus, its key, checked), which the palette (`Commands`) and the AI tools (`AxKitForm.ToolEntries`, for `axkit_window_commands` and `axkit_window_run`) both take; `Marked` gives an item its mark where it is made and `MarkOf` reads it (act when none was given, so the AI tools ask); `NeverForAiTools` withholds a menu and everything under it from the AI tools (AK-2.6, exception 3: the AI tools' submenu, AI help's switch and folders); `CommandMarkTests` keeps the number of unmarked items from growing.
  - `Commands` reads the window's menu bar into `AxKit.Core.PaletteEntry` lines (every enabled, available leaf item under a bar item, its name from `MenuText.Plain`, the menus as its path, the key from `ShortcutKeyDisplayString`, "checked"), each clicked by the window's one click, `ClickFor` (`notes/ai-and-automation.md`).
  - A submenu filled as it opens is `RefreshedOnOpening(menu, fill)` where its filling is attached, so that every reader fills it first (Open recent, the Window menu's lists); one too slow to fill for every reading is `NoEntry` (axFile's Drives, the Open with submenus), left out of the entries with everything under it, as is the one line that says a list is empty. **A new self-filling submenu is one of the two**, or the palette lists it empty or with last time's items.
  - `RefreshedOnOpening(menu, refresh)` attaches a refresh that changes items as the menu opens (axFile's Undo text, axTerminal's checked shell) and marks the menu so that `Commands` runs it before reading (2026-10-23).
  - The palette's flow is the window base's (`ShowCommandPalette` from `BundleKeys.CommandPalette` in `ProcessCmdKey` after the menu-mode check, and Help → Command palette, the first item `CreateHelpMenu` places):
    - a notice whose `OverlayInput` has `Changed` and `ListName`, so that `OverlayPanel` shows `_matches`, an `AxKitList` with its `Field` set, under the field (`SetMatches`, `MatchIndex`, `MoveMatch`, `MatchPage`);
    - the field's `KeyHook` moves through the matches;
    - the 300 ms `_paletteSpeak` timer says the first match;
    - Run is a `StaysOpen` choice that closes the notice itself and posts the entry's action, so that the focus is back where it was (the notice rule) before the command runs.
  - No window adds entries of its own since 2026-11-23 (the maintainer's word: commands only; axFile's folder entries and the hook for them went).

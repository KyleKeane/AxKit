# AxKit.Forms: the console window (axClaude, axTerminal)

Part of the notes of `src/AxKit.Forms` (the index is `src/AxKit.Forms/CLAUDE.md`). One bullet per piece.

- `ConsoleForm` (AX-6.5; docs/axTerminal/SPEC.md AT-2; 2026-10-23, docs/axKit/review-2026-10-23.md 5.1): the window of a console app on `AxKitForm`, what axClaude's `MainForm` and axTerminal's `TerminalForm` had twice. One class in partial files.
  - `ConsoleForm.cs`:
    - the controls (`Transcript`, `Input`, `Area`, `MenuBar`, `StatusBar` with `StateLabel` and `PlaceLabel`, `RecordItem`, `RecentFoldersItem`) made and docked in the constructor, which takes the app's `LineStore` model, its `TranscriptKeys` and the two accessible names; the field has a frame, a tinted background and its name in a header band over it since 2026-11-08 (a sighted helper took the white field under the white view for more of the conversation, the field and the view both being the white Window colour; the two are marked as the theme's classes field and band with `ThemePainter.Mark`, and the window base paints them with every other control, the tints of 2026-11-08 being the defaults; the name line follows the field's `Visible`, NVDA never reads it for the field), and `FitBottom` keeps it at three lines of its font and never past a third of the window on every font and size change, the control area's minimum following it;
    - the hooks a console window fills in (`Working` for the music, `OnTranscriptChanged` after the view, the status bar and the music have followed a change, `UpdateStatus` through `SetStatus`, `OnProcessExited`, `AskBeforeClosing` with `CloseConfirmed`, `SwitchFolder` for the recent folders, `ProcessConsoleKey` for a key of the window's that a command cannot say, axClaude's Ctrl+O guard);
    - the window base's hooks filled in for both (the chime from `SoundOnBell`, the hidden controls, `BottomControl` as the focus target, the view as `AnnounceSource`, `KeepCaret` from the control the notice opened from, the font, the update's and the settings error's system lines, `Fail` with a system line);
    - the shared commands (Tab, Shift+Tab, Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+Shift+Tab and F6 between the view and the bottom control, the find keys, the bookmark from anywhere) and the sealed `ProcessWindowKey` for Ctrl+Alt with a letter reading to the end, a pattern rather than a key;
    - `GoTo`, `CloseArea` (virtual: axClaude clears what it showed), the bookmark toggle;
    - the menu item helpers both windows place (`AddFindItems`, `AddBookmarkItems` with Next and Previous bookmark for Navigation, `CreateTranscriptContext` for the bar's Context: the line handed to the window, Bookmark this line with its key, Copy the selection, and `ContextFromControl` on the transcript and the field, AX-9.11, `TranscriptTextItems`, `CreateMusicItem`, which adds the music key).
  - `ConsoleForm.Process.cs`:
    - `StartProcess` (the pseudo console with the inbox posted once per burst, the recorder fed on the reader thread, the quiet timer, `LastOutputTick`; false after the error notice when it could not start);
    - the exit settle and `OnProcessExited`;
    - `StopProcess` (virtual: a window stops its own timers first);
    - `Post`, `Write`, `Enqueue` and `StartWrites` over the one paced queue with one pump timer (`_pump`; a restart stops it, so no second pump runs on, which happened until 2026-10-23), `ClearWrites`, `WritesPending`.
  - `ConsoleForm.Files.cs`:
    - `FindDialog` and `FindNext` over the base's Find notice and the view's search;
    - `Export` (the transcript as the reader sees it to a UTF-8 file);
    - the recording (`ToggleRecording` from the menu, `StartRecording` for axClaude's `--record`, the stop, the failure from the reader thread).
  - `ConsoleForm.Signals.cs`: `Signal` (the question or done chime, the alert in the background, the taskbar flash), `UpdateMusic` and `ToggleMusic`.
- `TranscriptView` with `TranscriptKeys` and `QuickKey` (docs/axTerminal/SPEC.md §5; from axClaude on its second use, axTerminal, 2026-10-18; `TranscriptKeys` carries `BookmarkLine` and `ReadToEnd` for the console window since 2026-10-23): the read-only transcript of a console (axClaude's conversation, axTerminal's terminal), a `TextBox` that applies `AxKit.Core.Transcript.TranscriptMirror`'s edits, keeps the caret on its line, holds output for 250 ms after a key press and for 4 s after a caret move from outside the view and outside a key's hold (NVDA's Say all moving the caret as it reads; `WndProc` on EM_SETSEL, `OwnSelect` for the view's own moves; axClaude FR-3.3, 2026-11-09), places the reading breaks, and adds the single-key navigation: the quick keys and their Ctrl+Alt layer (`ReadToEnd`), `l`, `m`, Backspace, Enter on a line (`LineChosen`), Escape (`EscapePressed`), Ctrl+C with a selection, find, the bookmark jumps and `JumpToLatestResponse`. The keys are the window's: it passes a `TranscriptKeys` record built from its own table (`AxClaudeKeys.Transcript`), the quick keys as `QuickKey` data (the letter, the kind's spoken name, which lines it stops at, a rule about the line before, and `ReadsWhole` for the paragraph key), so that each window keeps its letters and the collision test sees them. The gotchas of the EDIT control and NVDA's reading after a key press are in `src/AxClaude/CLAUDE.md`.
- `ControlArea` and `ListKeys`: one list or one field in the message field's place, named with the question, new for every question (axClaude D33). `ShowList`, `ShowField`, `Clear`, `Hold`, `FitHeight`; the lists are `ListBox` and `CheckedListBox` (NVDA read a `ListView` as a table). `ShowList`'s optional letter hook (2.11.0) is asked about a plain letter before the list's own jump, so that a screen's named key (d to discard) acts from anywhere in the list. `KeyHold` is the one-second hold on the answering keys that the area and the notice panel share (`Start`, `Clear`, `Holding`): a control that appears while the user types must not take the next keys as its answer.

# AxKit.Forms: the console window (axClaude, axTerminal)

Part of the notes of `src/AxKit.Forms` (the index is `src/AxKit.Forms/CLAUDE.md`). One bullet per piece.

- `ConversationForm` (docs/axKit/framework.md section 4.1, step 9, 2026-11-22): the conversation window's shape alone, on `AxKitForm`, split out of `ConsoleForm` unchanged so that a window without a program in a console can have it (axCalc, whose model is a `LineStore` it adds lines to itself; for it `TranscriptView` gained `CaretLineText`, the line you are on, which a command acts on without the line going anywhere). It holds what the bullets below say of the controls, of the window base's hooks, of the shared commands, of `GoTo`, `CloseArea` and the bookmark toggle, of find (`FindDialog`, `FindNext`) and of `AddFindItems`, `AddBookmarkItems` and `CreateTranscriptContext`; its constructor takes the model, the keys, the two names and whether the markers show; it asks the window for `OnTranscriptChanged` and `UpdateStatus` alone, and calls `OnModelSynced` between the status bar and the window's own pass, where the console window updates its music. It knows no process, no recording, no music, no folder and none of `ConsoleSettings`. `ConsoleForm` derives from it and keeps the rest.
- `ConsoleForm` (AX-6.5; docs/axTerminal/SPEC.md AT-2; 2026-10-23): the window of a console app, on `ConversationForm` since 2026-11-22 and on `AxKitForm` until then, what axClaude's `MainForm` and axTerminal's `TerminalForm` had twice. One class in partial files. The parts of the shape named below are `ConversationForm`'s now.
  - `ConsoleForm.cs`:
    - the controls (`Transcript`, `Input`, `Area`, `MenuBar`, `StatusBar` with `StateLabel` and `PlaceLabel`, `RecordItem`, `RecentFoldersItem`) made and docked in the constructor, which takes the app's `LineStore` model, its `TranscriptKeys` and the two accessible names; the field has a frame, a tinted background and its name in a header band over it since 2026-11-08 (a sighted helper took the white field under the white view for more of the conversation, the field and the view both being the white Window colour; the two are marked as the theme's classes field and band with `ThemePainter.Mark`, and the window base paints them with every other control, the tints of 2026-11-08 being the defaults; the name line follows the field's `Visible`, NVDA never reads it for the field), and `FitBottom` keeps it at three lines of its font and never past a third of the window on every font and size change, the control area's minimum following it;
    - the hooks a console window fills in (`Working` for the music, `OnTranscriptChanged` after the view, the status bar and the music have followed a change, `UpdateStatus` through `SetStatus`, which adds ", field in navigation mode" to the state while the field is in navigation mode (AX-8.13; the field's `ModeChanged` calls `UpdateStatus`), `OnProcessExited`, the field's and the session's hooks of `ConsoleForm.Field.cs` and `ConsoleForm.Session.cs` below, `ProcessConsoleKey` for a key of the window's that a command cannot say, axClaude's Ctrl+O guard);
    - the window base's hooks filled in for both (the chime from `SoundOnBell`, the hidden controls, `BottomControl` as the focus target, the view as `AnnounceSource`, `KeepCaret` from the control the notice opened from, the font, the update's and the settings error's system lines, `Fail` with a system line);
    - the shared commands (Tab, Shift+Tab, Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+Shift+Tab and F6 between the view and the bottom control, the find keys, the bookmark from anywhere) and the sealed `ProcessWindowKey` for Ctrl+Alt with a letter reading to the end, a pattern rather than a key;
    - `GoTo`, `CloseArea` (virtual: axClaude clears what it showed), the bookmark toggle;
    - the menu item helpers both windows place (`AddFindItems`, `AddBookmarkItems` with Next and Previous bookmark for Navigation, `CreateTranscriptContext` for the bar's Context: the line handed to the window, Bookmark this line with its key, Copy the selection, and `ContextFromControl` on the transcript and the field, AX-9.11, `TranscriptTextItems`, `CreateMusicItem`, which adds the music key).
  - `ConsoleForm.Process.cs`:
    - `StartProcess` (the pseudo console with the inbox posted once per burst, the recorder fed on the reader thread, the quiet timer, `LastOutputTick`; false after the error notice when it could not start);
    - `ProcessRunning` (virtual since 2026-11-17, for a window that can also run its program another way than in this pseudo console, axClaude's stream source, docs/axClaude/stream-mode.md SM-3.5; what the base asks about the program it asks there, the music's `UpdateMusic` included, never the host's field; no window fills it in yet);
    - the exit settle and `OnProcessExited`;
    - `StopProcess` (virtual: a window stops its own timers first);
    - `Post`, `Write`, `Enqueue` (with a check that is asked right before the write goes, false dropping it and what waits behind it: axClaude binds a message's and an answer's keys so, 2026-11-27; since the fifth review of that day the check answers an `AxKit.Core/Pty/WriteCheck`: goes, not yet, which keeps the write first and everything behind it waiting and asks again after `NotYetMs`, over, which drops it and all behind it and calls `WritesDropped`, the hook a window takes its unsent writes back in, as `ClearWrites` does, and skip, which drops it alone; since the sixth review of that day what the queue does with each answer is `AxKit.Core/Pty/WriteQueue`'s, pure and unit tested, and the pump only does what its `Next` says; a write with an empty text is never written, a point where the queue waits on its check, which axClaude uses to keep a message in flight until Claude Code has taken it) and `StartWrites` over the one paced queue with one pump timer (`_pump`; a restart stops it, so no second pump runs on, which happened until 2026-10-23), `ClearWrites`, `WritesPending`.
  - `ConsoleForm.Field.cs` (docs/axKit/framework.md step 11, first part, 2026-11-22; from axClaude's `MainForm` and axTerminal's `TerminalForm`, which each had the same handler, command and steps under other names): the field's keys, wired once as the window is made (`WireField`). Enter in the field sends and Shift+Enter is the field's own new line, Escape there is a guard that only speaks, and Ctrl+Enter is a command that sends as Enter does; a key of the app's own in the field comes first (`OnFieldKey`: axTerminal's history). Enter on a line of the view takes the line into the field (`TakeLine`): refused with the app's words while something stands in the field's place, or that closed for it (`LineRefusedWhileAreaOpen`), then the caret at the end, the words said and the focus in the field. `AddPlaceItems` puts the places on Alt+1, 2 and 3 (their older Ctrl keys went on 2026-11-26) at the top of the app's Navigation menu under the app's three labels; while the app's `PlacesRefused` gives words (axTerminal's screen view, its AT-9.10), the three say them and move nothing (`GoToPlace`), and so does `TakeLine`, which the Context menu still reaches then (2026-11-27). What stays the app's: `SendField` (what is sent), `SendFieldName` (the command's name), `EscapeGuardText` (what the guard says) and `WithLine` (the field's text with the line in it, and what is said: axClaude quotes the line between its markers and says its number). The keys are `ConsoleKeys` (`Send`, `Guard`, `GoToField`, `GoToView`, `Latest` and their older keys), which each console app's own table names for its window.
  - `ConsoleForm.Options.cs` (step 11, second part, the same day): the two Options submenus both console windows built over the same settings under their own words. `CreateSoundsMenu(chimes, ticks, music, musicKey)` is Sounds: the chimes, the ticks for new lines, and the music with its key; `CreateDoneMenu(name, announceDone, set)` is "When … is done": the word "Done", whose switch each app keeps in its own settings under its own name, the taskbar button's flash and the louder alert in the background. The labels that name the program are the app's.
  - `ConsoleForm.Session.cs` (step 11, third part, the same day), with `ConsoleQuestion` (a title, a text and the label of the button that leaves things as they are): the question before closing, the restart with its question, and the switch to another folder, whose steps both windows went through with their own words. `ClosingQuestion` gives the question or null to close now; the window shows it with Close anyway (`CloseConfirmed`) and the app's keep button, which after Update and restart keeps the installed update (`KeepWorkingAfterUpdate`). `RestartProgram` is the restart command: `RestartQuestion` first while the app has one, then "Restarting" and `RestartNow`, the app's own relaunch with its system line. `PickFolder` is the bundle's folder picker under the app's `FolderPickerText`; `SwitchFolder` checks that the folder is there, has the app keep it (`SetFolder`), says "Folder name" and has the app start the program there (`StartInFolder`). When a question is asked and what it says stay the app's, since only the app knows what its program is in the middle of.
  - `ConsoleForm.Files.cs`:
    - `FindDialog` and `FindNext` over the base's Find notice and the view's search, virtual since 2026-11-25 so that axTerminal searches its screen view while one shows (its AT-9.10);
    - `Export` (the transcript as the reader sees it to a UTF-8 file);
    - the recording (`ToggleRecording` from the menu, `StartRecording` for axClaude's `--record`, the stop, the failure from the reader thread; since 2026-11-25 a window fed otherwise than by its pseudo console records what it reads through `Recording` and `Record`, into a file whose kind it names by overriding `RecordingFile`: axClaude over Claude Code's stream, its lines as a `.jsonl` file; a write that fails never leaves `Record`, since 2026-11-27: the recording stops and says so, as from the console's own read loop; the line "Started ..." goes into the log with a session's id masked, `Redaction.WithoutIds`).
  - `ConsoleForm.Signals.cs`: `Signal` (the question or done chime, the alert in the background, the taskbar flash), `UpdateMusic` and `ToggleMusic` (on each window's `ToggleMusic` key, Ctrl+M since 2026-11-15, when Ctrl+Space became the bundle's mode key).
- `TranscriptView` with `TranscriptKeys` and `QuickKey` (docs/axTerminal/SPEC.md §5; from axClaude on its second use, axTerminal, 2026-10-18; `TranscriptKeys` carries `BookmarkLine` and `ReadToEnd` for the console window since 2026-10-23): the read-only transcript of a console (axClaude's conversation, axTerminal's terminal), a `TextBox` that applies `AxKit.Core.Transcript.TranscriptMirror`'s edits, keeps the caret on its line, holds output for 250 ms after a key press and for 4 s after a caret move from outside the view and outside a key's hold (NVDA's Say all moving the caret as it reads; `WndProc` on EM_SETSEL, `OwnSelect` for the view's own moves; axClaude FR-3.3, 2026-11-09), places the reading breaks, and adds the single-key navigation: the quick keys and their Ctrl+Alt layer (`ReadToEnd`), `l`, `m`, Backspace, Enter on a line (`LineChosen`), Escape (`EscapePressed`), Ctrl+C with a selection, find, the bookmark jumps and `JumpToLatestResponse`. `Sync` checks itself (axClaude FR-3.3a, 2026-11-15): the control's length before the first edit and after each, and the caret after each, which the control leaves behind what it put in; `OnGotFocus` compares the whole text with the mirror's (`FirstDifference`). On a difference `PutWholeText` logs one ERROR line of numbers (twenty a run at most; an automated run fails on it) and sets the control's text to the mirror's `Rendering` in one message; the caller puts the caret back. That is the one place that sets the text whole: a frame is never shown that way (it wraps every row again), and the checks must stay single messages that copy nothing, the one at the focus aside. The keys are the window's: it passes a `TranscriptKeys` record built from its own table (`AxClaudeKeys.Transcript`), the quick keys as `QuickKey` data (the letter, the kind's spoken name, which lines it stops at, a rule about the line before, and `ReadsWhole` for the paragraph key), so that each window keeps its letters and the collision test sees them. The gotchas of the EDIT control and NVDA's reading after a key press are in `src/AxClaude/CLAUDE.md`.
- `ControlArea` and `ListKeys`: one list or one field in the message field's place, named with the question, new for every question (axClaude D33). `ShowList`, `ShowField`, `Clear`, `Hold`, `FitHeight`; the lists are `ListBox` and `CheckedListBox` (NVDA read a `ListView` as a table). `ShowList`'s optional letter hook (2.11.0) is asked about a plain letter before the list's own jump, so that a screen's named key (d to discard) acts from anywhere in the list. Its optional `typed` (2026-11-26, axClaude's question) is called for every typed character but Space (letters, digits, punctuation), in place of the list box's own jump, with the digits of `ListKeys` off in that list (Page Up, Page Down, Home, End and the arrows stay), and does nothing while the area holds its keys: axClaude reads the question again with it, raised at the door as `EventKind.QuestionReadAgain`. Nothing jumps to an item by typing in that list (the maintainer's word of the same day, "every key reads the question"; a first version had kept the digits, y, n and an item's first letter jumping). The area's list is the one plain list box `ListTests` allows, with that reason. The name and the items are shown through `DisplayText.VisibleLine` by the area itself (2026-11-27), so that nothing stands unseen in what any window asks.
- **The hold, read this before changing how a question appears** (docs/axKit/SPEC.md AX-6.4, "A question that arrives by itself holds its answering keys"). `KeyHold` is the one-second hold on the answering keys that the area and the notice panel share; since 2026-11-27 it is `AxKit.Core/KeyHold` (`Start`, `Holding`, the clock handed in), where the rule is written once, and it has no `Clear`: a hold that runs is never shortened, and `KeyHoldTests` fails on a member that could end one early. In `ControlArea` every control shown holds: Enter, Escape, Space and typed letters and digits in a list, Enter and Escape in a field (what is typed goes into the field, so that the words typed after "Your words next" lose nothing). `ShowList` and `ShowField` still take `hold`, and it must be written out in the call: `hold: true` for everything a program or the window asks, whatever the user did a moment ago, and `hold: false` only for a control the user opened with a command of their own (Go to line, an editor's offer, axTerminal's recent commands), which `ControlAreaHoldTests` lists by file; it fails on any other caller and on a hold that is worked out (axClaude's was "the user did not act lately", and the question after an answer took a doubled Enter unheard, the review of 2026-11-27). One hold for each control, new with the control (the coordinator's correction of the same day: the next control inherits nothing; inheriting what was left of the three seconds after an answer made every list that followed the user's own key take Enter three seconds late, in silence, and bought nothing): a second from when it is shown, and what `Hold` adds once its own answer went, which only ever lengthens that control's hold; `Holding` says whether the shown control's keys are held; a control that a command run for an AI tool opened is held by `AxKitForm.RunForMcp` whatever its own code says. Beside the hold, two rules that `AnswerKeys` shares with the notice panel. A repeat never answers: the area's list, its list with check boxes and its field are classes of the area's own (`AreaList`, `AreaCheckedList`, `AreaField`) whose `ProcessKeyMessage` hands every key message to `NoteKey`, which notes whether it is the repeat of a key held down (`AnswerKeys.IsRepeat`, bit 30 of the key-down message); `Answering` takes the note once and swallows a repeat of Enter and Escape, and in a list of Space and of every letter and digit (a screen's own letter sent its key again and again until the third review of 2026-11-27), held or not, without a sound; a list's typed character (`KeyPress`) is read the same way. A held key is heard: a fresh press of an answering key that the hold takes calls `AnswerKeys.HeldBack`, the kind `Refused` raised at the door, at each press. The area's second constructor takes the clock and what a held key calls, for `ControlAreaHoldTests`, which presses keys in the real control without a sound. Every question of axKit is to start on its confirming answer (the maintainer's decision of 2026-11-27): these rules are then the one protection against a doubled, held or typed-ahead Enter, so never add a way to show a question that bypasses the area or a notice, a parameter that switches the hold off, or a call that ends one. Two rules since the third review of 2026-11-27 (`KeyHold`'s summary, 3 and 4), which a new way of asking has by being built on the area: the hold counts again from when the focus arrives (`FocusArrived`, on the control's GotFocus, for a control shown with `hold: true` or marked by `HoldAsArrived`, which the run for an AI tool calls; a control the user opened starts none), since axClaude put the focus on a question at the very moment its hold ended; and every callback that takes an answer passes `Takes` after its key was read, which refuses while the hold runs and for a control that is gone (`Fill`, the text an AI tool's run enters into the field that run opened, is the one call that passes none). Whatever a held list takes is heard, a punctuation mark and Shift with a letter included. `Arrived` says whether the control shown arrived by itself: `ConversationForm.CloseArea` asks it, and then holds the field's Enter for a moment as it puts the focus back there (`FocusFieldByItself`, `FieldTakesEnter`; a repeat of Enter in the field never sends, read in `ConversationForm.ProcessCmdKey`); so does `ConversationForm.FocusBackAfterNotice` after a notice that held its keys (the fourth review of 2026-11-27). Two more since that review: `FocusReturned` runs the window base's focus move back onto the control shown after notices the user opened from it (Explain, a help text) without starting its hold again (N4; the base's `FocusBackAfterNotice` is its one caller, which `ControlAreaKeyMessageTests` holds; not when the control still held its keys as the user left it, the fifth review of 2026-11-27, M2), and a tick of the list with check boxes inside the hold, by a click or UI Automation's Toggle, is set back in its `ItemCheck` and heard (L7). `ControlAreaKeyMessageTests` plays all of it with the key messages Windows sends (`KeyMessages`, in the tests). What is still open: whether the sound of a held key stays is the maintainer's to hear.
- `ConversationPlaces` (docs/axKit/workflow-graph.md WG-1.1, 2026-11-26): the conversation window's places, `text` (the `Transcript`) and `field` (the `Input`, a waypoint), given by `ConversationForm.Places` for axClaude, axTerminal and axCalc; the `ControlArea` in the field's place is what is over it (`choice`), never a place.

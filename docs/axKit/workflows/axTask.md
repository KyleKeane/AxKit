# axTask: workflows

Written by `tools/workflows.ps1`; never edit it by hand. Every goal below is played by axKit's automated checks as it
is written here: the steps are what the check presses and types, and the lines under a step are what axKit says. A
line "You hear" gives words of what axKit says there, not always the whole sentence; after "words such as" they are
cut short. After "Type:" comes the text to type, to the end of the line, with no full stop added. The file names and
the words typed are the check's examples: use your own. Where a step names a command after its key, the key is that
command's in this version of axKit.

### Hear that a field to type in opened

1. Press Ctrl+F (Find in the list).
    You hear the sound FieldOpen.
2. Press Escape.
3. Press F2 (Rename; Context menu).
    You hear the sound FieldOpen.
4. Press Escape.

### Hear that a form opened, and its explanation only on demand

1. Press Ctrl+M.
    You hear the sound FormOpen.
    You hear: Move Fix the tap, form.
2. Press Ctrl+Q (Read in full; Context menu).
    You hear: Now in Home. Up and Down choose the list.
3. Press Escape.

### Turn the entry sounds off and on

1. Choose Entry sounds in the Options menu, Text.
    You hear: Entry sounds off.
2. Press F1 (Keyboard shortcuts; Help menu).
3. Press Escape.
4. Choose Entry sounds in the Options menu, Text.
    You hear: Entry sounds on.
5. Press F1 (Keyboard shortcuts; Help menu).
    You hear the sound TextOpen.
6. Press Escape.

## Filter and sort a list of axTask

Starts with: Open axTask with the folder tasks.

### Narrow the lists to those whose name contains a text

1. Choose Filter in the Options menu.
2. Type: shop
3. Press Enter.
    You hear: filter shop, 1 shown.

### Put a list's items in the order of their names

1. Type: h
2. Press Right.
3. Choose Name in the Options menu, Sort by.
    You hear: Sorted by name.
4. Choose The list's own order in the Options menu, Sort by.
    You hear: Sorted by the list's own order.
5. Close the window.

## Hear the end of a list that holds one item or none

Starts with: Open axTask with the folder tasks.

### Hear both ends of a list of one item

1. Press Down 2 times.
2. Press Right.
3. Press Up.
    You hear the sound Edge.
4. Press Down.
    You hear the sound Edge.

### Hear that a list with no item has nowhere to go

1. Press Left, Up.
2. Press Right.
3. Press Up.
    You hear the sound Edge.
4. Press Down.
    You hear the sound Edge.
5. Press Home.
    You hear the sound Edge.
6. Press End.
    You hear the sound Edge.

## Add an item to a list without going into it

Starts with: Open axTask with the folder tasks.

### Add an item to a list from the top level

1. Press Ctrl+N (New item; Actions menu).
2. Type: Paint the door
3. Press Enter.
    You hear: Added Paint the door to Home.

# axTemplate: workflows

Written by `tools/workflows.ps1`; never edit it by hand. Every goal below is played by axKit's automated checks as it
is written here: the steps are what the check presses and types, and the lines under a step are what axKit says. A
line "You hear" gives words of what axKit says there, not always the whole sentence; after "words such as" they are
cut short. After "Type:" comes the text to type, to the end of the line, with no full stop added. The file names and
the words typed are the check's examples: use your own. Where a step names a command after its key, the key is that
command's in this version of axKit.

## Make, write, rename and delete a template in axTemplate's browser

Starts with: Open axTemplate.

### Make a template

1. Press Ctrl+N.
2. Type: Thanks
3. Press Enter.
    You hear: Added Thanks.

### Write the text of a template

1. Press Enter.
2. Press Down.
3. Press Enter.
4. Type: Thank you, {clipboard}.{caret}
5. Press Ctrl+Enter.
6. Press Down.
7. Press Space.
8. Press Down.
9. Press Escape.

### Rename a template

1. Press F2 (Rename).
2. Type: Thank you note
3. Press Enter.
    You hear: Renamed Thank you note.

### Delete a template and bring it back

1. Press Delete.
2. Press Enter.
    You hear: Deleted Thank you note.
3. Press Ctrl+Z (Undo; Actions menu).
    You hear: Undone: Deleted Thank you note.

### Rename a template from its Title field

1. Press Up.
2. Press Enter.
3. Type: Greeting
4. Press Enter.
    You hear: Renamed Greeting.
5. Press Escape.

## Delete several templates, and meet a file that is no template, in axTemplate's browser

Starts with: Open axTemplate.

### Delete several templates at once

1. Press Home.
2. Press Space.
3. Press Down.
4. Press Space.
5. Press Delete.
6. Press Enter.
    You hear: Deleted 2 templates.
7. Press Ctrl+Z (Undo; Actions menu).
    You hear: Undone: Deleted 2 templates.

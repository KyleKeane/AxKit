# axHelp: workflows

Written by `tools/workflows.ps1`; never edit it by hand. Every goal below is played by axKit's automated checks as it
is written here: the steps are what the check presses and types, and the lines under a step are what axKit says. A
line "You hear" gives what axKit says there; "something with" and words in quotes give a part of it. After "Type"
the text to type stands in quotes: the quotes and the full stop after them are not typed. The file names and the
words typed are the check's examples: use your own. Where a step names a command after its key, the key is that
command's in this version of axKit.

## Ask Claude for help from anywhere, with a picture of the screen

Starts with: Open axHelp.

### Turn on the AI tools

1. Choose Let AI tools read and open axKit in the axKit menu, AI tools.
2. Press Enter.
    You hear: AI tools on.
    You may hear: Not in a test run.

### Open a conversation with Claude

1. Press Enter.
2. Go to the window that opened.
    You may hear: trust.
3. If that came: Press Enter.
    You hear: Ready.

### Hand a picture to a new conversation

1. Open axHelp again with the file blocks.png.
2. Go to the window that opened.
    You may hear: trust.
3. If that came: Press Enter.
    You hear: Ready.

### Quit axHelp

1. Go back to the first window.
2. Choose Quit axHelp in the Session menu.
3. Go to the window that opened.
4. Press Tab.
5. Press Enter.

## Close axHelp's conversation and bring the same one back

Starts with: Open axHelp. Go to the window that opened.

### Hide the conversation

1. Close the window.

### Bring the same conversation back

1. Go back to the first window.
2. Choose Open the conversation in the Actions menu (key: Enter).
3. Go to the window that opened.
    You hear: Unsent message in the field.
    You hear the sound Recall.

### Hand a picture to the hidden conversation

1. Press Ctrl+A, Delete.
2. Close the window.
3. Open axHelp again with the file blocks.png.
    You hear: Text added to your message from another window, not sent.
